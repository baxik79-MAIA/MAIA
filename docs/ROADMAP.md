# Roadmap

MAIA is under active development. This roadmap states direction, not delivery dates or guarantees.

1. Maintain the public source baseline through reviewed public-safe core changes, deterministic validation and clear separation from machine-local operational state.
2. Strengthen the existing local intelligence and advisory product slice with observable failures, bounded behavior and clear evidence/provenance.
3. Continue capability-boundary and deployment-lock verification, including MAIA operating with Round Table absent.
4. Develop additional useful work capabilities only through accepted specifications, policy/approval boundaries, implementation and acceptance evidence.

The workspace already contains core/runtime/store foundations, optional consultation adapters, experimental desktop/host applications and local intelligence diagnostics/recording. Their presence does not establish production readiness or general support across platforms/models.

Broad connector automation, packaging, sophisticated routing and commercial production readiness require additional acceptance work. CPU-first/local-first operation, human control and provider neutrality remain constraints. Source publication does not cross the existing company/customer-data gates.
