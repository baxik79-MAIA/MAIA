# MAIA Product Direction Universal Commercial Benchmark Victor

**Architecture Owner directive.** This is a forward-only product direction
amendment. It does not rewrite accepted M0.0-M0.5 history, create an ADR, or
alter canonical YAML contracts.

## Direction

MAIA is potentially commercial software for multiple classes of users. It is a
universal, local-first intelligent work system with one provider-independent
core. MAIA owns canonical state and memory; models are replaceable
computational resources. Deterministic tools perform deterministic work, while
external frontier AI is consultation capacity under policy and approval.

Victor is a competitive and technical benchmark, not a clone target, fork or
architectural dependency. Product progress must be demonstrated through
capabilities and benchmarks rather than unsupported superiority claims.

## Binding principles for forward work

1. MAIA is potentially commercial software.
2. MAIA serves multiple user and workspace classes, not one specific user.
3. MAIA remains local-first and useful with external providers disabled.
4. MAIA is provider-independent.
5. MAIA owns canonical state and memory.
6. Models are replaceable computational resources.
7. Deterministic tools perform deterministic work.
8. External frontier AI is consultation capacity.
9. Human approval remains available for consequential actions.
10. Victor is a benchmark, not an architectural dependency.
11. Prefer mature permissively licensed components to needless reinvention.
12. Every reused component needs provenance, license and security review.
13. Product knowledge must be reconstructable without AI chat history.

## Scope and non-authorizations

This direction authorizes narrative documentation, benchmark research and
commercial-readiness recording. It does not authorize importing Victor code,
adding a model/provider dependency, changing a canonical contract, modifying
approval or privacy policy, or starting a new product milestone without
Architecture Desk review.
