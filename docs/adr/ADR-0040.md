# ADR-0040: Reasoning Assurance A0-A4 and selective Round Table

Status: Accepted by Architecture Desk for M0.7

## Decision

Introduce ReasoningAssuranceLevel A0 deterministic, A1 single model, A2
independently verified reasoning, A3 Round Table and A4 human decision required.
It is separate from existing RiskClass, ApprovalAssurance and ApprovalGate.
Selection considers impact, ambiguity, uncertainty, conflicting evidence,
novelty, reversibility and policy.

Round Table is selective, not default. A3/A4 require independent reasoning
contributions, disagreement recording, evidence comparison, adjudication,
provenance and usage/cost recording. A3 grants no execution privilege. A4 adds
human confirmation of a high-impact reasoning conclusion; it does not replace
ApprovalGate when a downstream Action independently requires approval.

## Consequences

Reasoning confidence cannot silently lower action risk or authorize a side
effect. The contract does not select a provider, model or runtime architecture.
