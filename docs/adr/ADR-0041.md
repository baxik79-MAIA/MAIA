# ADR-0041: Commercial readiness and measurable product advantage governance

Status: Accepted by Architecture Desk for M0.7

## Decision

Tenant, Membership and Entitlement are commercial/platform seams. Personal or
local Workspaces may exist without a Tenant. Commercial organizational mode
requires the Tenant seam and fails closed if absent; M0.7 creates no multi-tenant
runtime infrastructure or Core pricing logic.

Measured Advantage requires an instrumented metric or named benchmark for
successful Outcome rate, human review/correction effort, cost per successful
Outcome, TTFV, TTFA and repeatability effort. Unknown cost or human effort
remains unknown, never zero. TTFV begins when authorized usable context exists
and excludes application opening or generic model output. TTFA excludes
diagnostic, no-op, setup and test actions and never weakens approval rules.

G2 is the pre-commercial governance alert FORM COMPANY NOW before the first
paid production pilot, recurring B2B contract or external-customer production
data processing beyond a controlled non-production demonstration. G3 Funding
Readiness and G4 Scale Capital are strategic non-runtime gates. MAIA is neither
a legal nor investment adviser, and legal form remains a founder/legal/accounting
decision.

## Consequences

Commercial claims become falsifiable without putting pricing, legal choices,
fundraising decisions or G2/G3/G4 into Task or Action FSMs.
